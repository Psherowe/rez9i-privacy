1. Introduction

REZ9I is a personal finance application that helps users record income and expenses and calculate their balance.

2. Information and Local Storage

REZ9I does not require an account or login. Financial records entered into the app are stored locally on the user's device and are used to provide the app's features.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

The app's core functionality does not transmit financial records to a developer-operated server. We do not sell users' locally stored financial records.

If third-party services or software libraries are included in the app, their data practices must be assessed and disclosed where applicable.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Records remain on the device until deleted by the user or removed with the app. Uninstalling the app may permanently delete locally stored records. Users should keep their own backups of information they wish to retain.

5. Security

Users should protect their devices with a screen lock and keep their operating systems updated. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

REZ9I is a personal finance tool and is not specifically designed to collect children's personal information.
